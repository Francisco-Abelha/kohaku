Last updated 13 November 2026.

Kohaku is a concept store: a portfolio project, not a real business. It sells nothing, ships nothing and has no customer accounts. This page explains the little that happens to your data when you visit.

## What we collect

Nothing, directly. The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. It doesn't know who you are and doesn't follow you between pages or sites.

## Forms

The contact and newsletter forms are demonstrations. Submitting them shows a note on the page; what you type is not sent anywhere and not stored.

## Fonts and images

Fonts and images are served from this site's own address. Your browser makes no requests to Google Fonts or other third-party services to display the pages.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Netlify and Vercel. Like any web host, they keep standard server logs (such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested) to run and protect their service. They handle those logs under their own privacy policies.

## Checkout

There is no checkout yet. When a test checkout is added, payments will run through Stripe in test mode: card details go to Stripe, never to this site, and no money moves. This page will be updated before that happens.

## The code

The site's source code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/Francisco-Abelha/kohaku), so anything described here can be checked there.
